    IUCN Red List

    Ocean giants, still fragile

    1. Ship strikesBusy shipping lanes cross feeding grounds. Speed limits clearly reduce deaths.
    2. EntanglementLost and active fishing gear injures thousands of whales every year.
    3. Underwater noiseWhales hunt and talk with sound. Ship and sonar noise drowns out their world.
    4. A warming oceanKrill swarms are moving, and whales follow their food into new and riskier routes.
